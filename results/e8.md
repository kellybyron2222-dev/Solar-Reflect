# E8 — Optimal satellite count

**Campaign.** After E7. Same civil-night snapshot. No new \(I^*\). Close-out of the fleet question: given \(T^*\), \(h\), and \(I^*\), what \(N\) is, and which \(h\) (and \(A\)) minimizes it.

## 1. Question

What formula gives the number of satellites for a required brightness and duration, and where is that number smallest?

## 2. Formula

Rectangle model (peak \(I\) held for \(T_\text{useful}\)). One site.

\[
N(A,h)=\frac{T^*}{T_\text{useful}(h)}\,\max\!\left(1,\frac{I^*}{I(A,h)}\right)
\]

Coded as `fleet_count`. Equivalent: target fluence \(F^*=I^*T^*\) and per-sat fluence \(F=I\,T_\text{useful}\),

\[
N=\frac{F^*}{F}\quad\text{while }I<I^*,\qquad
N=\frac{T^*}{T_\text{useful}}\quad\text{once }I\ge I^*.
\]

**How to minimize \(N\).**

1. **Foil area.** Raising \(A\) lowers \(N\) until \(A=A_\mathrm{req}(I^*,h)\). Past that, extra area does not buy minutes. So \(A^*=A_\mathrm{req}\) if unconstrained; \(A^*=A_\max\) if the sheet cannot grow.
2. **Orbit, unconstrained \(A\).** Then \(N^*=N_T=T^*/T_\text{useful}(h)\). \(T_\text{useful}\) rises with \(h\). Higher is fewer trains, at the cost of a larger sheet. No interior minimum.
3. **Orbit, fixed \(A\).** Minimize \(N\) \(\Leftrightarrow\) maximize \(F=I(A,h)\,T_\text{useful}(h)\). Brightness falls with height; duration rises. That trade has an **interior minimum** on \(h\).

Civil night is impossible below \(h_\min\approx 449\,\mathrm{km}\) (\(\theta(30^\circ)=6^\circ\)). 400 km is below that floor.

## 3. Unconstrained foil (\(A=A_\mathrm{req}\)) — \(N=N_T\) only

\(\eta=1\). \(A_\mathrm{req}\) is C-light (\(0.1\,\mathrm{W/m^2}\)); \(N_T\) does not depend on \(I^*\).

| \(h\) | \(T_\text{useful}\) | \(N(20\,\mathrm{min})\) | \(N(3\,\mathrm{h})\) | \(A_\mathrm{req}\) for \(0.1\,\mathrm{W/m^2}\) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 449 km | \(\to 0\) | \(\to\infty\) | \(\to\infty\) | floor |
| 625 km | 2.809 min | 7.120 | 64.08 | \(1.84\times 10^4\,\mathrm{m}^2\) |
| 1000 km | 5.757 min | 3.474 | 31.27 | \(2.02\times 10^4\,\mathrm{m}^2\) |
| 1500 km | 9.209 min | 2.172 | 19.55 | \(2.92\times 10^4\,\mathrm{m}^2\) |
| 2000 km | 12.57 min | 1.591 | 14.32 | \(4.27\times 10^4\,\mathrm{m}^2\) |
| 3000 km | 19.40 min | 1.031 | 9.278 | \(8.10\times 10^4\,\mathrm{m}^2\) |

One satellite can hold **20 min** near **3000 km** if the foil is already \(\sim 280\,\mathrm{m}\) on a side. Three hours still needs a train at every LEO height in this table.

## 4. Fixed foil — interior optimum

Merit \(F/A=I(1\,\mathrm{m}^2)\,T_\text{useful}\) peaks at **\(h\approx 1420\,\mathrm{km}\)**. Same neighborhood minimizes \(N\) for M18 / M55 / undersized M1km.

**C-light** (\(0.1\,\mathrm{W/m^2}\), \(20\,\mathrm{min}\), \(\eta=1\)):

| \(h\) | M18 \(N\) | M55 \(N\) | M1km \(N\) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 625 km | 404.8 | 43.36 | 7.120 |
| 1000 km | 217.0 | 23.24 | 3.474 |
| **1500 km** | **196.1** | **21.00** | **2.172** |
| 2000 km | 209.7 | 22.46 | 1.591 |
| 3000 km | 257.8 | 27.61 | 1.031 |

M18/M55: **1500 km is best** (about half the 625 km count). M1km is already bright enough, so it follows the unconstrained column and prefers still higher.

**C-weak** (\(50\,\mathrm{W/m^2}\), \(20\,\mathrm{min}\)), M1km still undersized:

| \(h\) | \(N_T\) | M1km \(N\) |
|---|---|---|
| 625 km | 7.120 | 65.58 |
| 1000 km | 3.474 | 35.16 |
| **1500 km** | 2.172 | **31.76** |
| 2000 km | 1.591 | 33.97 |

Same bowl: **~1500 km**.

## 5. Check cited

- `fleet_count` at 625 km M18 C-light = E7 \(404.8\).
- Sized \(A=A_\mathrm{req}\) recovers \(N_T=7.120\).
- \(N(1500\,\mathrm{km})<N(625)\) and \(<N(2000)\) for M18 C-light (test).
- \(h_\min=449\,\mathrm{km}\) from \(\theta(30^\circ)=\theta_\text{night}\).

## 6. Observation

If you can grow the foil, the fewest satellites is just the train \(T^*/T_\text{useful}\), and higher orbit always wins until one pass is long enough. If the foil is stuck at 18 m or 55 m, **do not sit at 625 km**: ~1500 km cuts the street-class count by about half. You cannot beat \(N_T\) without a longer pass. 400 km is not in the feasible set.

**Not:** Earth coverage. Not GEO. Not a cost optimum (larger \(A\) and higher \(h\) both cost). Not stacked suns.
