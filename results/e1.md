## 6. Observation

The lit patch is the Sun’s image. Its width tracks altitude: \(D=h\alpha\). Its area is \(\pi(D/2)^2\), tens of square kilometres on the v1 grid, \(\sim 26.5\,\mathrm{km}^2\) at 625 km. A ground target smaller than that image cannot be filled as a hard-edged spotlight; more collecting area \(A\) brightens the same patch and does not shrink it. Focusing at \(f=h\) does not reduce \(D_{\min}\). GEO is a size check (\(D\sim 333\,\mathrm{km}\)), not a campaign point.
