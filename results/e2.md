## 5. Check cited

- **C3.** M55, 625 km, ideal: hand \(0.1097\,\mathrm{W/m^2}\). Kernel \(0.1097\,\mathrm{W/m^2}\), rel \(0.011\%\) (gate 1%). Realistic \(0.07406\,\mathrm{W/m^2}\) matches the sheet.
- **C1 identity.** \(I\times A_\text{image}=E_0\eta A\cos\gamma\) at the snapshot.

## 6. Observation

Peak \(I\) is collected sunlight spread over the solar image from E1. It scales as \(A/h^2\) because \(A_\text{image}\propto h^2\), not because the foil is a lamp. At 625 km, M55 is \(0.1097\,\mathrm{W/m^2}\) ideal and \(0.07406\,\mathrm{W/m^2}\) with \(\eta=0.675\). A dish with \(f=h\) does not change that centre value. At M1km and 400 km, \(A\) is \(9.2\%\) of \(A_\text{image}\): the filled-image formula is then an approximation (the foil’s own size starts to matter). The kernel is not switched.
