# E3 — Required collecting area

**Campaign.** E3 of E. Not a verdict. Not a kernel change. Invert of E2, not a new model.

## 1. Question

What collecting area \(A\) is required for each parked \(I^*\) versus altitude \(h\)?

## 2. Inputs

| Item | Value |
|---|---|

\(A/A_\text{image}=I^*/(E_0\eta\cos\gamma)\) does not depend on \(h\). Height only scales the absolute area as \(h^2\). For \(I^*=1000\,\mathrm{W/m^2}\), \(A_\mathrm{req}>A_\text{image}\) even at \(\eta=1\) (\(E_0\cos\gamma\approx 962\,\mathrm{W/m^2}\)). That 962 is the small-\(A\) formula at fill, not an étendue proof that C-day is unreachable. The kernel is not clipped. \(A_\mathrm{req}\) matches **centre** \(I^*\); mean over the growing \(A_\text{lit}\) is lower (F12). At C-weak fill \(0.052\), \(I_\text{mean}/I_\text{peak}\approx 0.66\); at C-energy fill \(0.208\), \(\approx 0.52\).

## 6. Observation

Required area is the E2 brightness law run backwards. At fixed \(I^*\), \(A_\mathrm{req}\propto h^2\). The fraction of the solar image you must cover, \(A/A_\text{image}\), is set by \(I^*\) versus \(E_0\eta\cos\gamma\), not by height. Duration \(T^*\) is not in this table: a larger foil does not buy hours. C-day (\(I^*=1000\)) inverts past \(A_\text{image}\); that is still the linear formula, not a new kernel.
