## 6. Observation

Useful overhead access is a few minutes on this grid (\(4.281\,\mathrm{min}\) at 625 km; \(2.786\) at 400 km; \(13.25\) at 2000 km). Horizon time is longer; the period is an hour and a half or more. A larger collecting area does not change any of those numbers. Fluence \(F_\mathrm{pass}=I_\text{peak}T_\text{useful}\) is an **upper bound**: the kernel holds \(I\) at the nadir peak for the whole window, and it does not cut umbra. One satellite does not supply a three-hour \(T^*\). Covering \(3\,\mathrm{h}\) at 625 km is a multiplier \(\approx 42\), not a longer pass.
