# Report figure captions

Each takeaway is within the F5 claim it supports. Nadir plots are the envelope, not the night score.

## S-geo-2d

- **Takeaway:** The 45° is at the foil. The center ray meets the local ground at 90°.
- **What is plotted:** Two-panel schematic at terminator-nadir, \(h=625\,\mathrm{km}\): fold (\(i=r=45^\circ\), \(\varepsilon=90^\circ\)) and solar-image width \(D=h\alpha\).
- **Assumptions:** Flat foil; sun at infinity; local ground is the tangent.
- **Pointer:** F5.1; E1; O1.

## S-focus

- **Takeaway:** A dish focused at the ground does not shrink the solar image. Extra curve opens \(D\).
- **What is plotted:** 2D schematic: flat, \(f=h\) (\(D_\min\)), and over-bent. M55 side is an illustration.
- **Assumptions:** Terminator-nadir; geometric optics; extended Sun.
- **Pointer:** F5.1; E1; O3.

## S-spot-h

- **Takeaway:** Patch width is height times how wide the Sun looks. At 625 km, \(D=5.812\,\mathrm{km}\).
- **What is plotted:** \(D\) vs \(h\) from 300–2000 km. Grid ticks at 400, 625, 1000, 2000 km. GEO is a callout, not an axis point.
- **Assumptions:** Nadir, \(D=h\alpha\), \(\alpha=0.0093\,\mathrm{rad}\) full diameter. Small-foil kernel (no \(+w\)).
- **Pointer:** F5.1; E1; hand sheet.

## S-I-A

- **Takeaway:** Peak \(I\) scales with collecting area over the solar image. It is not a lamp \(1/r^2\).
- **What is plotted:** Peak \(I\) vs \(A\) at 400, 625, 1000, 2000 km. Solid: kernel peak. Dashed: mean \(I\) on the growing sun-shadow (F12). Markers M18, M55, M1km. Two panels: \(\eta=1\) and \(\eta=0.675\). No \(I^*\) horizontals.
- **Assumptions:** Terminator-nadir envelope. Centre \(I\) is the kernel until fill ~1.
- **Pointer:** F5.2; E2.

## S-dwell

- **Takeaway:** Useful light is minutes, not hours. Area does not buy the window. Literature “~20 min at 1000 km” is the horizon pass, not \(T_\text{useful}\).
- **What is plotted:** \(T_\text{horizon}\) and \(T_\text{useful}\) vs \(h\) for an overhead track (\(\varepsilon_\min=30^\circ\)).
- **Assumptions:** Geometric access; no umbra; no Earth rotation. Night off-track windows are shorter (E6: 2.809 min at 625 km).
- **Pointer:** F5.5; E4; F13.
